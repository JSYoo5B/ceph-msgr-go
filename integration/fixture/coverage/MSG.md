## Product wire gaps

These are gaps in the Go implementation on this area's paths; the
[index](README.md#product-wire-gaps-separate-from-environment-completeness)
says how they differ from environment completeness.

- Secure-mode compression and zlib are deferred
  ([compression plan](../../../internal/msgr/COMPRESSION_PLAN.md)).
- MON, MGR and OSD sessions do not implement cookie resume; this is a
  documented contract boundary, not a gap.
