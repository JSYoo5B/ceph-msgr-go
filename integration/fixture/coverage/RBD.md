The index states the classes, how to read the columns and the selection

## Updates

Updated on 2026-11-13 for issue #12. RBD-7 is new: one Go journal append
whose reply is lost while the appender holds the image's exclusive lock, with
two dedicated catalog rows and the record
[RBD_JOURNAL_LOST_APPEND_VALIDATION.md](../RBD_JOURNAL_LOST_APPEND_VALIDATION.md).
The visibility columns of RBD-5 and RBD-6 now point to it. RBD-6 stays
`proposed` for the remaining fault combinations. No class changed.

Updated on 2026-11-13 for issue #12. RBD-8 is new: the exclusive lock taken
from an owner that never releases it, once from a Go owner whose watch
expired (native librbd blocklists it and breaks the lock) and once from a
killed native owner (the Go client blocklists it and breaks the lock), with
two dedicated catalog rows and the record
[RBD_LOCK_OWNER_LOSS_VALIDATION.md](../RBD_LOCK_OWNER_LOSS_VALIDATION.md). The
visibility columns of RBD-3, RBD-6 and RBD-7 now point to it. RBD-6 stays
`proposed` for the remaining fault combinations. No class changed.

Updated on 2026-11-13 for issue #12. RBD-9 and RBD-10 are new. RBD-9 is the
lost journal append of RBD-7 on three replicated BlueStore OSDs, with a
change of the acting primary while the outcome is unknown and one explicit
resubmission of the original transaction to the new primary; two dedicated
catalog rows, the only RBD rows on three OSDs, and the record
[RBD_JOURNAL_PRIMARY_CHANGE_VALIDATION.md](../RBD_JOURNAL_PRIMARY_CHANGE_VALIDATION.md).
RBD-10 is the owner loss of RBD-8 with two requests of the first owner held
unanswered on the OSD through both handoffs; two dedicated catalog rows and
the record
[RBD_LOCK_OWNER_LOSS_PENDING_VALIDATION.md](../RBD_LOCK_OWNER_LOSS_PENDING_VALIDATION.md).
The visibility columns of RBD-6, RBD-7 and RBD-8 now point to them. RBD-6
stays `proposed` for the remaining fault combinations. No class changed.
